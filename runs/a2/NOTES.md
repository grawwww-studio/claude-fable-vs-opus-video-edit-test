- Made: `out/speedups-vs-face-short.mp4`, 1080x1920, about 49.9 s, from `work/edit.yaml` (`bin/ed verify`: check, lint, defaults all ok). Chain: `work/t.yaml` (treatment) -> `bin/ed treat` -> `work/t.events.yaml` -> `bin/ed py work/finish.py` -> `work/edit.yaml`. `work/fixwords.py` writes the corrected word files in `work/words/`.
- Story: hook "Fewer steps should mean fewer seconds" over two result clips labelled 600 s vs 147 s (AI video and the topic are on screen from frame 0). Then "It's not that simple", who she is, what was tested and why the face matters, the 8/6-step LoRAs (267 s, 208 s), close vs wide at 4 steps, the four faces side by side with their seconds, what was not measured, and the point "check the face, not just the clock". It ends on a short card: 4.1x as fast, still check her eyes, full episode on the channel.
- Left out: take08 ("Here's what I'd actually use"), because the material never says which setting she would use, so it would promise something the Short can't deliver. Also cut the "You might know her" line from take03, because it assumes the viewer knows the channel.
- Captions: the recogniser's mistakes are fixed from TAKES.md (Mae, one RTX 4090, MiniMax H3, speed-ups, lip-sync). The spoken word is cyan, not yellow, because identity C says yellow is never a text colour.
- All numbers on screen come from TAKES.md: 600, 267, 208, 147, 2.2x, 2.9x, 4.1x, 20/8/6/4 steps.
- Tried and dropped: (1) `broll` result clips (aiclip). These add a second "AI-GENERATED" label that cannot be switched off, which the brief forbids, and it also failed the lint. (2) Light panels with clip tiles. The clips came out as tiny thumbnails and covered the presenter's face (lint L2). (3) Dark-ink kickers, which were invisible on her dark wall. (4) The `titlecard` and `card` end cards, which overflowed the 9:16 frame; the card also printed a studio name and colour bars. I used grid shots and a light panel on a dark cut instead.
- Sound: her voice only, mastered by the editor. No music bed was added. The result clips are silent. I ran `bin/ed words` on the rendered mp4 and every line is heard, including under the grid shots. One render was used, out of the four allowed.
